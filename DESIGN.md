# Cleo Days — Android PWA
## Design
- Floating, thumb-friendly bottom navigation; Egypt-local live day/time and accessible search.
- Responsive safe areas, system font, optical type, high-contrast backgrounds, meaningful press feedback.
- Reduced-motion and reduced-transparency preferences respected.
- Standalone install with maskable icons and manifest shortcuts.
- Offline-first service worker caches the app and all 14 original resort photographs.
- Local-only favorites, plans, and export/import. No login or analytics.
## Source
The holiday source and all 14 photos came from the original user-supplied Cleo Days V2 archive. Extra claims are not presented as verified unless supported.
